## 2026-07-27 — wiped and re-scaffolded as multiplatform Swift

The Objective-C app that lived here (Parse-backed agricultural calendar,
Crashlytics, 108 `.m`/`.h` files) was wiped at the owner's instruction. All 20
of its commits remain in this repo's history and on `origin/master`; nothing
was force-pushed.

In its place: a multiplatform Swift scaffold on the dark-factory skeleton.
`HengeCore` + `HengeUI` as SwiftPM libraries, iOS and macOS app targets
generated by xcodegen from `project.yml`, sharing `App/` and the bundle id
`io.river.henge`. Verified green from cold: `make test` runs 5 tests in about
a second with no simulator, `make build` succeeds warning-clean on both
platforms.

**Blocking everything else: `MISSION.md` is not written.** Per
`factory/dark-factory.md` §3.1 the repo is therefore not yet a dark factory —
it is Level 3, agent proposes and owner decides. The scaffold deliberately
contains no domain code, so nothing has to be un-guessed later.

**Next:** owner writes the mission (ROADMAP M0). Then M1 replaces the
`SemanticVersion` placeholder in `HengeCore` with the first real domain type.

**Not yet decided, for whoever picks this up:** whether the branch stays
`master` (inherited from 2014; every other river.io repo uses `main`), and
whether henge keeps its old agricultural-calendar subject or takes the name in
a new direction.
