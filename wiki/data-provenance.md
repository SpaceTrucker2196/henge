# Data provenance

The authoritative registry lives in `SECURITY.md` (invariant 5: data
arrives with provenance settled first). This page is the readable summary;
if the two ever disagree, `SECURITY.md` wins and this page has a bug.

| Dataset | Source | Licence | Where |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain | Environment Agency LIDAR Composite DTM 2022, 1 m bare earth, via Defra's WCS | Open Government Licence v3 — © Environment Agency copyright and/or database right 2022 | `salisbury-plain.heightfield`, baked by `scripts/bake_terrain.py` |
| Stone plan | Tim Daw, *stonehenge-block-3d*, `locked_poses` — 93 stones digitised from the M J Rees & Co 1989/90 survey, Historic England Archive MP/STO0861, each with Daw's accuracy grade | CC BY-SA 4.0 — the derived CSV is published under the same licence; decision in `docs/decisions/2026-09-23-stone-data-licences.md` | `stones/daw-locked-poses.csv`, written by `scripts/import_daw_poses.py` from a pinned commit. Positions and yaw for standing stones, outlines for what lies; placeholders ship as *provisional* |
| Stone heights | Cleal, Walker & Montague 1995, *Stonehenge in its Landscape*, Appendix 5 "Heights of Stones", pp 547–548 (1919 Chief Architect's report and Atkinson) | ADS Terms of Use: research reuse including the commercial sector, with credit | `stones/cleal-1995-appendix5.csv`, transcribed from the page images; standing stones take the single figure, stones re-erected in 1958 are recorded as they lay and keep the fallback |
| Stone heights, 1877 | Petrie 1880, *Stonehenge: Plans, Description, and Theories*, pp 9–12 | Public domain | `stones/petrie-1880-heights.csv`; the independent pre-restoration check, compared with Cleal by a test |
| Star catalogue | Hipparcos, ESA SP-1200 (CDS I/239) | Free with attribution | `hipparcos-bright.csv` (8,870 stars, V ≤ 6.5) |
| Star names | IAU-CSN (WGSN, E. Mamajek) | Official IAU nomenclature, freely published | generated into `StarCatalog.properNames` |
| Constellation figures | **Authored in-repo** — no external figure set consulted | Ours | `ConstellationLines.swift`, generated by script |
| Planetary theory | VSOP87D (CDS VI/81), truncated | Free scientific data | `VSOP87Tables.swift`, generated |
| Moon colour map | NASA SVS CGI Moon Kit (LRO) | Public domain | `moon-albedo.jpg` |
| Surface textures | ambientCG Rock030 / Grass004 | CC0 1.0 | engine resource bundle |
| Sky model | Preetham et al. 1999 | Published formula, no data vendored | implemented in MSL |
| Algorithms | Meeus; Espenak & Meeus ΔT | Published methods, cited | implemented in `HengeAstro` |
| Milky Way | NASA SVS Deep Star Maps 2020 (SVS 4851), from Gaia DR2 | Public domain; credit NASA/GSFC SVS and ESA/Gaia/DPAC | `milkyway-2020.jpg`, 2048×1024 |

Declined or waiting on licence: Hosek–Wilkie sky data,
any recorded ambient sound, Stellarium-style constellation datasets
(mooted by drawing our own), H. A. Rey figure drawings (same).

Rule of the house: **no dataset enters the repo before its licence is
written down here**, and the first third-party *code* dependency is a
stop-and-ask.
