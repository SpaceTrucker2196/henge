Henge is a 3D model of Stonehenge driven by a real astronomical ephemeris. No account or sign-in exists; the app makes no network requests. The free tier gives full access in 15-minute sessions; a single non-consumable purchase (io.river.henge.full, $4.99) removes the session clock and unlocks the date-travel calendar. The paywall is reached from the lock pill at the top right, or automatically when a free session ends.
